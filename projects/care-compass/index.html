02 / METHOD

Two measures.
No blended score.

GP APL estimates local service availability after accounting for nearby communes, practitioner activity and age-weighted need. Specialist data measures straight-line distance from a mairie to the nearest listed consulting site.

01
Align on official commune codes

Join on INSEE commune code. Keep codes as text; never match places by name alone.

02
Respect the correct population weight

Use standardized population for APL averages; use total 2022 population for distance shares.

03
Check the publisher's summary

Blank nearest-site distance counts as over 30 km. Recomputed commune counts match the published synthesis.

04
Keep the fee categories separate

Compare Sector 1 / health centre, Sector 1 + OPTAM, and all conventional sectors.

03 / READ WITH CARE

What these data
cannot tell us.

These are territorial indicators for exploring patterns, not a directory or a clinical decision tool.

01

Distance is measured as the crow flies from the mairie; it is not a road journey or travel time.

02

The source does not state whether a practitioner accepts new patients or how soon an appointment is available.

03

Public-hospital outpatient specialist clinics are not represented in the specialist-distance measure.

04

Fee categories do not show an individual's bill or actual affordability; non-contracted doctors are excluded.
